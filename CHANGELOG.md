# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first cut

The queue, the window, the two lids, the re-derived tier, day mode, and a report
that reads `gh pr list` and `git log` instead of nightwatch's own record.

Pulled out of a private rail that ran nightly against one repo for eight nights.
The one thing that changed on the way out: the tier is re-derived from config on
every look, rather than trusted from the item.
